Who are we?

Hey! We’re Yuliia and Artem — a couple creating Votum while keeping up with our energetic one-year-old.

We’ve spent years working in commercial IT — from coding to managing projects — and now we’re diving into our biggest challenge yet: building our own platform!

The idea for Votum started with a simple question: what do people really think? Like:

- What's better — pizza or burgers?

- Does social media do more harm than good?

- Was your work affected by AI?

We wanted a place for honest, snappy opinions — from light laughs to deep debates because knowing what people really think helps us question our beliefs and start conversations that matter.

We built Votum from the ground up — and the iOS app is almost here.

Hop in, vote on everything, and turn thoughts into conversations.

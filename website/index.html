Who are we?

Hi, we're Julia and Artem, and we've been building Votum together since April 2025 while also raising our toddler.

Votum is a place where anyone can create polls and vote to see what others really think about anything. From serious topics like politics or the cost of raising kids, to random stuff like "Do you brush your teeth before or after breakfast?"

We started Votum because it feels like real opinions get lost online. Everything is loud, extreme, or trying too hard. We wanted something more honest and way simpler.

We can't wait to bring Votum to life and finally see what the silent majority actually thinks.
